## Hi, I'm Nitin 👋

AI Research Engineer at **NPCI** (National Payments Corporation of India). I work on **post-training LLMs** — preference optimisation and RL with verifiable rewards — RL environments for tool-using agents, and **evaluating LLM safety and reliability** in high-stakes, multilingual domains.

### Publications & research
- **FiMI** — post-training and agentic integration of NPCI's 24B financial LLM, which powers UPI Help and MyUPI · [arXiv:2602.05794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05794)
- **FiMI Banking** — preference optimisation and verifiable-reward RL for safe banking agents · [arXiv:2609.03960](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03960)
- **IndicBankBench** — a benchmark for the safety and reliability of banking LLM assistants · [arXiv:2609.29167](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29167) · [code](https://github.com/npci/IndicBankBench)
